01 / Why Solar

Why Now Is the Right Time to Switch

Electricity costs in Kashmir don't stay flat. Between rising per-unit tariffs and the unpredictability of grid supply — especially through winter months — your monthly bill is one of the few household costs that keeps climbing without warning.

Solar changes that equation. Once installed, your rooftop system generates power directly from sunlight — including on cloudy days, when panels still draw usable energy from diffused light. Any surplus you generate is credited back to your account through net metering, offsetting what you draw from the grid during low-sunlight hours.

You're not just cutting a bill. You're fixing a cost that would otherwise keep rising for the next 25+ years, in a state where almost nothing else gets cheaper.

What net metering actually credits

The barrier has never been the technology. It's the process — paperwork, subsidy approvals, and finding someone accountable to see it through. That's what we handle, start to finish.
